# Business Model: Product Graders and Testers Sample-Intake Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-7543`
- ISCO-08: `7543`
- Occupation: Product Graders and Testers (excluding Foods and Beverages)
- Social impact: product-safety, quality-assurance, consumer-protection

## Customer

- manufacturing/industrial quality-control labs / grading houses
- independent grader/tester crews / testing-lab cooperatives

## Offer

- grader/tester session shift scheduling coordination
- sample-intake/batch-identifier record logging
- testing-supply order coordination (test equipment consumables, sample containers)
- sample-condition anomaly surfacing to the human grader/tester

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no performance of the grading/testing evaluation itself, ever
- no direct recording or finalization of a quality-grade assignment,
  ever
- no direct recording or finalization of a pass/fail determination,
  ever
- no finalization or authorization of a quality clearance, ever
- flagged sample-condition anomalies (visible defect, out-of-tolerance
  reading reported by intake staff) always route to human sign-off,
  regardless of confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
